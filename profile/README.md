# 🇵🇱 Polish Language School

**Polish Language School** is an AI-powered ecosystem providing end-to-end relocation and academic support for students moving to Poland. 

By merging an advanced AI learning platform with offline mentorship, we ensure a seamless transition at every step of your journey.

## 🎯 Key Focus Areas

* **Comprehensive Polish Training:** Structured courses from beginner to advanced levels tailored for academic and everyday fluency.
* **State Exam Prep:** Smart tools and practice modules designed specifically for official Polish language certification exams.
* **Consul Interview Simulations:** AI-driven practice sessions to prepare confidently for Karta Polaka and visa interviews.
* **Full Admissions & Relocation Guidance:** End-to-end assistance with Polish university applications, paperwork, and settling in.
